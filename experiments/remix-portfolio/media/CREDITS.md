The Sun, striped planet, blue planet and rocky planet are shaded, recolored orthographic projections of the Sun, Jupiter, Neptune and Mars maps from [Solar System Scope](https://www.solarsystemscope.com/textures/), used under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). The lunar surface uses the existing [NASA LRO Moon Kit](https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/4720/) projection from the first preview.
